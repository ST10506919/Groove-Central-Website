About Groove Central Vinyl

Our Story

Groove Central Vinyl was started in 2025 by a Johannesburg-based collector who began trading vinyls out of a single crate at weekend pop-up markets around Johannesburg. Demand grew quickly, and has now flourished into a business with a permanent storefront in Randburg, supported by an online presence to reach buyers beyond the immediate area.

Our Mission

To reconnect the people with the tactile, analogue experience of music by supplying authentic, well-curated vinyl, tapes and CDs.

Our Vision

To become Gauteng's most trusted independent all-things-music destination, both in-store and online.

Who We Serve

Collectors and audiophiles, younger listeners drawn to the current vinyl revival, older collectors rebuilding their collections, and local music lovers seeking imports.
